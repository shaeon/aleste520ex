                              Translation Notes

The two so-called "1533xxx" chips are (similar) to russian "K555xxx" chips,
maybe 1533 series and 555 series refer to western 74LS and 74HC variants or so.
  1533LI1 = K555LI1 = 74LS08 = Quad AND gates
  1533TM2 = K555TM2 = 74LS74 = Dual flipflop
Expressions like "D41/11" do refer to Pin 11 of chip D41 in the schematics.
